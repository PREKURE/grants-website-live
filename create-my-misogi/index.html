What's a Misogi?

Have you ever done something so HARD it defined your entire year?

Ran a marathon. Started a business. Ended one. Quit drinking. Faced something you’d been avoiding for years.

Enter Misogi: The ancient Japanese Shinto ritual of purification through extreme challenge. Traditionally, it’s about cleansing the body and spirit under icy waterfalls. But today, it’s evolved into a yearly rite of doing one thing so brutally challenging, it redefines your limits and echoes through the other 364 days.

Does the following sound familiar?

“I’ll only have two biscuits tonight instead of x.”

“I’ll cut back on wine for…(insert arbitrary feel-good time frame).”

“But this week has been hard because…(insert external blame here).”

 

You’ve said it. We all have. In a world that peddles easy fixes like pills and procedures first, Misogi flips how we set goals and think about health. It’s about choosing hard to unlock your potential.

It’s time to set a goal that scares the shit out of you. Yes, we are no longer just talking and thinking about doing hard things. It’s time to take action.
